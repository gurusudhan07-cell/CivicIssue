The problem

Cities do not lack complaints. They lack a way to tell which one matters most.

📨

Scattered reports

Calls, messages, forms and social posts reach different desks, so one pothole is logged ten times.

⚖️

No triage

A flooded junction beside a hospital waits in the same line as a faded road marking.

🕳️

No visibility

Residents never learn what happened, so trust falls and fewer people report.

How it works

From a photo to a fixed road in four steps

STEP 1

Snap

A resident photographs the issue and drops a pin or uses GPS.

STEP 2

Understand

Vision AI names the issue, rates severity and rejects non-issues.

STEP 3

Prioritise

Votes, age, rainfall and nearby schools or hospitals set the rank.

STEP 4

Resolve

The owning department closes it and the audit trail is public.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is the data real?

The map, hospitals and schools, address lookup and weather are live open data. The complaint tickets in this prototype are simulated so every feature can be explored.

How does the AI work?

The photo goes through a secure serverless route to a vision model, which returns the issue type, severity and a one-line description. Photos that are not civic issues are rejected.

How is priority calculated?

Severity, citizen votes, complaint age, nearness to a hospital or school, and live rainfall for drainage issues are added into a score out of 99.

Can it work for another city?

Yes. Only the locality list and department mapping are city specific.
